About PrimeFrame

A clearer way to present property.

PrimeFrame_Real Estate Visual Studio helps property professionals create clean, engaging marketing visuals. Our approach combines careful image enhancement, transparent virtual staging and cinematic editing.

We aim to respect the real property: no invented rooms, misleading structural changes or concealed defects. Virtual furnishing concepts are presented as digital staging, not as the property's current condition.
